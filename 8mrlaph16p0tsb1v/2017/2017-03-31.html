15: 5:00 PM

Worried about losing all my free time, which I haven't really enjoyed. It's become clear that aimlessness is not for me. Yet my desire for it is still there. I'm having a hard time making sense of it all. Depression would be an easy answer. So it feels. It's likely true that when I'm happy (not low, depressed) these things don't bother me, simply because they are not there. Regret, distortion, lost opportunities, lack of big sweeping changes. My mind is scattered and aching.

I don't feel passionate about my work. I've been close to convincing myself... convince being the key word. Writing feels good, but in this state I can't get off the ground. Ideas stop at ideas and in no time fade away and are forgotten despite being etched onto some digital page.

I feel guilty as a human being. Sick. Tired. Reticent with love and the emotions that strengthen bonds. They are there, held down by a worrisome weight, watching the world drift by without their longed for participation. Crys go unnoticed at my sickness's smug behest.
