Date: January 1, 2017 at 00:00:00 AM NST



### 1: 7:34 AM
Hello 2017.

My prediction for the year: good one for me, bad one for the world. I'll take it? …


### 1: 1:39 PM
I'm a bubble gum kid --- Ts great intro at pp place. Highlight if the trip. 


### 1: 6:10 PM
Theodore was a crazy crazy kid when he got home. Tantrum about using R's phone. Had to run creme on his belly that he scratched up in his madness. 


### 2: 8:18 AM
Got frustrated with Marianne overnight. Made her cry uncontrollably. Feel awful about it. Note it, fix it, and move on. 


### 2: 8:42 AM
Just said bye bye to Marianne as she was taking Kanan for a stroller ride. She walked back towards me saying kiss kiss and pecked me on the cheek. Sweetest thing. 


### 2: 2:26 PM
Received first issues of Economist and New Yorker today. Will this be another in a long string of failed hobbies/routines? The first Economist already has opened my eyes to some interesting world issues (possible and existing niqab/burqa bans) including the death of the founder of vexillology, Whitney Smith. Interesting, exceedingly strange, importantl, life. 
 I have 12 week trials of both magazines. The hope is to reduce screen time with print media. It's not as if my life depends on knowing this information in the most timely fashion. The appeal of not getting sidetracked by pundits and fake news is appealing. Let's see how this goes. 


### 2: 2:36 PM
In Buenos Aires teachers get promoted on merit not based on union structure. Is the ease of obtaining a degree and the influence of unions hurting our kids? According to a small piece in the Economist, we are doing things wrongly; at least so far as our kids are concerned.  


### 2: 2:51 PM
What makes me feel great? 
Yoga 
Reading
Board games
Being present with the kids
Being in communication with friends. 
Being productive with school work and work work. 


### 2: 5:50 PM
I'm going to put pretend ketchup on your real head. - Theodore Milley. 


### 2: 5:51 PM
I put pretend ketchup all over Marianne's real face.  - Theodore Milley. 


### 2: 5:52 PM
2nd quote above is the more accurate quote


### 2: 6:54 PM
Question to consider: How can we incorporate solar energy into our own home? 


### 2: 11:07 PM
Tonight:
Read some Economist (day one!)
Read a little A Christie (and then...)
Played 7 Wonders w/ Pantheon
Got high and watched the Americans. 
It's been fun to watch a series together. Tense show. 


### 3: 8:34 AM
As promised, showed T a video of Apollo 11 on the moon. Also watched Chris Hadfield brushing his teeth on the ISS which I think he found fascinating. It's fun (and scary) to shape his little mind. 


### 3: 7:20 PM
Today was a good day. Rebecca and I sent the kids to CLs in order to get the house in order for back to work.   I read (novel and mag) and cooked (tortillas, tzatziki, chicken, pork, Brussels sprouts, potatoes).

Went to the gym this morning. The beginning of my unstructured 'program'. Did some squatting and 15m of running. Couple that with either yoga or 7 minute workout and I got 'er scald. 


### 3: 10:32 PM
I'm happy right now. I suppose it's important to say that from time to time. 


### 4: 7:09 AM
It's a new work year. This is the beginning of a change. I can feel it. It is felt. I am... tired. I am uncertain. I am going to give it a good try. 


### 4: 9:34 AM
New shows to check out (look at me being productive at work!): 
Horace and Pete
Better Things
Baskets


### 4: 1:24 PM
This form of journaling appears to be working well, but there are also some problems. The obvious being that I'm primarily journaling on the phone which is limited and prone to many many errors. If you are reading this, blame all errors on the phone. I guess I should just pull up the text file and add to it on a laptop / desktop... problem solved?


### 4: 4:14 PM
Long conference call: total waste of time, did not have the setup for it, frustrated. Let it go. Breathe in .. out. It is done. Over. Moving on. 


### 4: 4:18 PM
I can chose whether or not to let a small part of my day influence the mood of the rest. I am choosing to not let it. Continuing on. 

Good note: the tortilla wrap I had today was fantastic. All homemade. Go me!


### 4: 10:12 PM



### 4: 10:14 PM
I have limited time to read, but that is what I enjoy most, so I should make the most of the time I do allot myself (without unnecessary pressure). 


### 5: 11:43 AM
I may disagree with the way BUSN courses are being developed, but the work does allow for using my brain.. me against d2l in a way. I'll let this slide and continue on. I can still find areas to display my creativity and productivity. 


### 5: 12:14 PM
Started to read an interesting piece in the New Yorker last night about the nature of Time. Augustine is quoted often in the piece and has created a gap in my knowledge that needs filling. 

Almost finished And Then There Were None. Great story! Wargrave   was my guess for most of the novel, but not so much after his death... I thought it would be one of the earlier deaths, faked of course. Will have to go back and reread his Introduction to the book and the group. 


### 5: 4:41 PM
Just finished AC ATTWN. Turns out there is a recent Lifetime miniseries based on the book. Will have to check that out sometime. 


### 6: 6:53 AM
So tired. How can anyone genuinely like this stage of having kids. The missed sleep kills. (Being a little facetious, but still...)


### 6: 9:59 AM
Feeling down about my career. Need to make changes, even if it's only how I approach, feel, and deal with the parts I find frustrating. 


### 6: 1:19 PM
It'll do no good to treat this melancholy with purchases. You have everything you need. Buck up. Note the discomfort, frustration, stress, and move on. 


### 7: 9:09 AM
Picked up Zealot by Rex's Aslan yesterday at the library. I may have picked it up before as the reaction I've had after the first chapter evoked a faint déjà-vu. It's too combative. This is what I think based on all my credentials... there are others that think differently, but they are wrong. The argument is unconvincing and comes off as uncollegial. Think I'm going to put it down...  perhaps for the second time. 


### 7: 7:40 PM
Grace and forgiveness for myself. I need some more of that in my life. Sinner and saint. All of the time.
