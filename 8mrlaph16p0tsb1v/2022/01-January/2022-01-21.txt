# Sorry iA Writer

I foolishly left this app behind in the midst of a snowy December. Obsidian caught my eye and I was gone. 

Obsidian is now where I keep mobile notes, notes that build and grow over time (so called evergreen notes), and notes movies, video games and... oh I don't know. Why bother with notes at all? Some days, like today, I just don't know. Nevermind.

iA Writer encourages me to just write write write. Takes up my full screen without customization. I don't have to fiddle with settings to make it perform how a writing tool should.

Truthfully, I haven't taken advantage of that feature as much as I should. To write with abandon. To get words out of my brain quickly and onto the page. That's why typing + iA Writer allows me to do best.

That is all to say... perhaps I'll return here more regularly. I hope so.
