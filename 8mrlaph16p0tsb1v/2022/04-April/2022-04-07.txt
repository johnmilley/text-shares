# A new season

April is here. A lot of snow is still here and keeps coming. Warmer weather around the corner. I want to throw discs into metal baskets.

I will be 39 on Saturday. I am doing okay— ever on the road to doing a little better. 

The worst of the last fall is subsiding. I feel presence throughout the day. 

Healing.
