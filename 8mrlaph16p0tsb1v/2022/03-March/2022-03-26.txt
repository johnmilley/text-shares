# Welcome Wally

Wally joined our family last night. R and I drove out to the Deer Lake  airport and picked him up around 12:30am. A kindly looking man opened a corner door near the baggage carousel and placed his crate on the ground in front of me. I picked up the carrier and peered in to the sweetest little face.

He spent the drive to his new home comfortably in R's lap. He had a little nap. It was a bright dark night. The expected snow was just beginning as we rounded the last highway corner into town.

He slept overnight in the crate he was delivered to us in, in our room. Never made a peep (the same could not be said for all 3 kids, who wet their beds). 

The kids came into the room to meet him at 6:30, but were successfully shooed away until 7:00.

It is a snowy Saturday morning and he is sleeping in his crate—door open—in the kitchen.
