Date: December 1, 2016 at 00:00:00 AM NST



# December 2016

---- 21: 4:01 PM ----
Last night I was reading a short history of progress and came across an interesting fact about human usage of fire and the purposeful clearing of huge swaths of land such as the prairies and the Australian outback for agricultural purposes.  This is explained more in Loren Eiseley's "Man the firemaker"


---- 21: 4:06 PM ----
I tried things that I have done while happy in the recent past. I'm attempting to turn former effects into causes. Rebecca assures me that there is some psyc to back this up... is there a name for it? There must be. We name everything. 


---- 21: 4:10 PM ----
What makes me happy? 

I woke up today ready to meet the day. This hasn't happened in weeks. Last night I did some yoga, flossed, and had sex (2% chance...). 



### 21: 7:36 PM
Time to start thinking about a will and other end of life preparations.   If I were to pass very little of my writings, photos, and videos would be easily accessible. Some would be lost forever (to be fair, quite a lot of it deserves to remain in the dark)

Look into will kits.

Set up appointment with lawyer if necessary (from what I currently know, while it may not be necessary, it's definitely the smart thing to do... especially with kids involved)

Write letters (handwritten and digital) to Marianne, Theodore, Rebecca, Mom, Dad, Jennifer, Jill, and the grandparents. Tell them what they mean to me.


### 21: 7:37 PM
Mcsweenys... worth reading?


### 21: 7:43 PM
Mcsweeneys is Dave Eggers' publication. The only thing I know about him is a comment on his righteousness (or other such undesirable trait) from some random reddit user. It's time l update my uninformed opinion on the man. 

He really caught my eye with the head that is issue 37. I noticed it staring out from a periodicals shelf as I was closing up the library. 


### 21: 8:57 PM
I have been a little worried about my arm of late. That may be one of the reasons why I haven't been to the gym in sometime. Think I need to change up the routine for a time. Running and yoga. What does that body look like? Nothing stopping me from adding in some compound lifts (squat and deadlift especially)


### 22: 7:08 AM
Marianne marked up Theodore's face with an ultra washable marked this morning. Right below the eye.  


### 22: 7:33 PM
Related to my recent commitment to prepare for end of life, it may be prudent to enroll both myself and Rebecca in a First Aid course. Y'know... to prevent a possible untimely end. 


### 23: 7:49 AM
Oi... I wish I went to bed at a grownup time. Alone John needs a talking to. 


### 23: 9:59 AM
Assembled a dollhouse for the kids last night. Hoping to keep it a surprise for Christmas morning for everyone. I think Theodore is going to love it. 


### 23: 2:26 PM
The relationship Rebecca and I have is something to be cherished and looked after. Regular maintenance required. Rust protection. I'll take it all. Too many people are all alone and/or lonely. We have two wonderful (of often mischievous) children and a great understanding of each other. She knows my good and bad and accepts the whole package. She cares about improving my bad and I her's. We are two colours of Playdough mushed together that will never dry.  


### 23: 4:31 PM
These are good times. Bad times seem to pop in unannounced, leaving little or no time for turning off the lights and getting out of sight. Let these daily thoughts be close at hand in times of imminent danger. 

T quote (yelled out while I was in the bathroom):
Dad are you still sick?
Yeah
Oh. Me too.  


### 23: 7:59 PM
This is one of the worst hair cut experiences of my life. Lol


### 24: 5:57 PM
Nice Christmas Eve. This morning we went for a walk around the loop (half loop). We met Andrew May and his son Evan along the way. Evan gave Theodore a ride on his Norwegian kick sled (that was made by a man in Pynns Brook). Their dog, Blue, was putting along at a slow trot. 14 years old. 

Theodore and I then stayed out and played in the snow. Rolling, burying, snow angels, snow balls, sliding. All the usual fun stuff. Love seeing that joy on his face. Nothing else is on his mind but the fun at hand. One of the many moments that you long for childhood. Being able to foster that wonder is the next best thing. 

Got my hair cut by Elizabeth at Silver Scissors. A fine day-one cut. 

Cleaned off Rebecca's black Go stones that she'll be getting tomorrow morning.  Tedious work. 

Will be soon getting the kids into their Christmas pjs that Nanna bought for them. 

A fine day. 


### 25: 6:49 AM
It's Christmas morning. Marianne and I are the first ones up. I'm sure there will be earlier Christmas mornings in our future. Not bad! Patience of a priest needed today if yesterday is any indication. Let's do this. 


### 25: 8:53 AM
Well.  That went about as expected. Rip rip rip tantrum rip rip play sweetly wolf down lunch play tantrum.  Marianne is being adorable pushing her new stroller and doll. She's also played with the dollhouse without prompting. Theodore has played nicely and not so nicely with his BBQ. We are currently in the den watching paw patrol. He's dressed as Buzz Lightyear. It's not even 9 yet!  Happy and somewhat optimistic... I suppose that is the Christmas spirit talking. 


### 25: 11:51 AM
I lay down from mental exhaustion in the shallow snow bank. The birds flying in formation seem to know where they are going. I suppose at least one of them knows. I'd rather be les somewhere, even a dead end, then to be left standing still. 


### 25: 3:07 PM
Spent a little too much on board games... again. Rebecca is happy with her Go set. Was very very worried about that. In the car with her now with two sleeping children as she reads the rules. Hoping to try it out tonight. 


### 25: 3:11 PM
I did very well in the gift dept. 
granola bar baking tray
Tortilla press
Skillet for blini/crepes and tools to help with the tickness (not on the name of those yet)
Corn flour!
Started my LoA subscription with 3 Steinbeck books. 




### 25: 8:31 PM
Holy tired hell, batman.  Exhausting, at times frustrating, long,  long long long day. Long day. So long. Long.
Yeah... I'm a little tired. 

Wouldn't change a thing. A memorable day (maybe? guess that’s a silly thing to declare the day of!).


### 26: 9:07 AM
I have a cold this morning. The kids have picked up where they left off yesterday evening - running laps. 


### 26: 3:52 PM
Complete reversal of yesterday to no one else's fault. Woke up feeling sick and feeling lower and lower ever since.
