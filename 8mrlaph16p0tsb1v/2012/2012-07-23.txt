"Let's play the alphabet game."
"Which one?  The sign or the anything outside-the-car game?"
"The second one.  Asphalt."
"We're almost there.”
She points to the black SUV in front them.  "Bumper."
"Ok, I give up, you win."
"Car, Dirt, Evergreen..."
He gives in. "ATV, Broken line."
A look of protest quickly flashes, unseen, over her face.  The rules clearly state - surely someone must have written them down somewhere - that adjectives do not count.  Victorious, she smiles and pauses, giving him a chance to catch up.
