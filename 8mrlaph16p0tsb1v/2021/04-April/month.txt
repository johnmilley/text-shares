Need to right myself

Focus on the good things. My kids. My wife. They are the people who I want to know intimately for the rest of my life. They will always be my focus. Furthering that goal will be a part of any decision that I make. In my heart, I would not want it any other way.

My heart doesn't always win out. I am sorry for the times where greed and false desire take over. I buy things to feel differently. I know that and I continue to do it. It *can* be stopped.

I have fostered this poor part of my palette since middle school, where I first ventured into the theft business. The first items that I began covet were Calvin and Hobbes comic collections. When I was 12 or 13, a good friend gifted me a copy of *The Revenge of the Baby-sat*. For ... reasons ... a duplicate of the book had come into his possession. I loved the comics. The expressive drawings of Calvin hooked me. They continue to. That good friend, Bradford Crocker, taught me how to steal and the first lesson was Calvin and Hobbes comic collections from the local chain book store. They were easily stuffed up under puffy winter jackets and secured between elastic-waisted jeans and pubescent boys' bellies.

It changed who I have become as a person. That desire for the next thrill (in the form of product...) remains strong. Apple Air Pods, Reigning Champ Sweatshirt, Paint Supplies, Books. That's just this year. It's April.

I can stop. I will stop
