### A Habs fan

Theodore asked how long he had been a Habs fan tonight. I told him  that he just started this year. A great year to start. I believe I was around his age when I started buying hockey cards. That was my intro. MTL was an early favourite because they were my grandfather's favourite. Simple. Easy.

Colin's surprise response to my email (subject: how about, body: dem habs?) has got me thinking, about what it means to be a fan while knowing full well that you are practicing some form of fanaticism (He has not been following the team at all for years.). Some, myself included, think that being a fanatic of anything is a bad idea.
