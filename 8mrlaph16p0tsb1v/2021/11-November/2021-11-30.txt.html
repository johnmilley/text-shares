# November Brief Notes

## Brief Notes


2021-11-04  7:41 AM:  The first snowfall of the year. Theodore and Marianne yelled out for permission to go outside and experience it at 7am. Joyful.


2021-11-06  2:04 PM:  waiting in a line up for a double buddy chicken burger. The kids (M and T) are in the pool. I just threw 9 disc golf discs four times each up and down the high school field. My hands are warming up. 

I am now parked at the pool. Going to eat the double chicken buddy burger that and listen to Courtney Barnett sing _if you gotta go go go go_




2021-11-06 11:53 PM:  trying hard not to break down: a memoir. 

I lack courage. I lack the courage for authenticity. 

Why can't I just let myself be?




2021-11-06 11:59 PM:  I have a friend. But I don't have friends like I once did. Your father is friendless, kids. Don't let it happen to you. 

Live a little more freely. Follow your urges—the options that make you brain-happy and body-scared. 

In love, I have been blessed. Your mother is my everything. 


2021-11-08  7:57 AM:  long-billed dowitcher. That's the name of the bird we saw that day during a walk at the cabin. Before kids. Just the two of us. That dowitcher did not want us around. 

Nope. Fred corrected me. It was a much more common greater yellowlegs. 


2021-11-08  9:28 PM:  Hit on at Walmart (a working title). Man in his 40s with brilliant old blue eyes questioned if I might join him for tea near the super hero and Star Wars toys at Walmart. His name was Wayne. For 2 or 3 minutes, Wayne commanded my attention. Intentionally. It was intense, but pleasant. He had kind eyes and seemed harmless enough, but yeah, I was taken aback. He engaged in good natured small talk... the more I think about it the more conflicted I am about it. Walmart is a strange place to pick someone up. Maybe he wasn't who he said he was. Maybe he knew to bring up Burgeo/Ramea. God. I'm paranoid. That's great. Should not have smoked tonight. Going to blight this with some mindless entertainment.


2021-11-08 10:53 PM:  Judge Dredd was that bandaid. Sweet movie-healing power. This film is fucking great. Good for them! ... ok this is just my intimate reaction, don't pay close attention to that. I will present my final thoughts TOMORROW. duhn duhn duhnnnnnn...


2021-11-09  8:14 AM:  paranoid me now assumes that this guy (Wayne may have been the first name to come to his mind after hearing mine) was a cop. Asking to see my face at the end... confirming if I was his target. Maybe I wasn't and that's that. Strange that he brought up Ramea and Burgeo. 

This is what it feels like to be prey.



2021-11-09  1:28 PM:  there is a smokiness in the air. Hole 4 at Blanche Brook. I love this sport. 

Just played 2 rounds at the new place. +1 and -1. Feels good to be improving at something in my life, however inconsequential that something may be. 


2021-11-09 11:20 PM:  An interesting day

Decided late last night to cancel this mornings class. 

- Spent the hours of 8–9:40 at Corner Brook Tire waiting on a wheel alignment and oil change.
- Went home and picked up L and R for their respective drop-off locals. Returned home. Splashed water into my face, brushed my teeth, and packed for a half-day on the road. Stephenville-bound.
- Pulled up to the new DG course ~11:30am. Darryl Chipp pulled up as I was choosing my discs for the round. I agreed to play with him and his buddy, Fred. Darryl was a pleasure to play with. Explained the course layout/design/future etc. Kudos to him for getting this course set up. It is a beauty. A joy to play.
- After 2 rounds (one with Darryl and Fred +1(maybe +2), one solo -1), I headed to Blanche Brook for a round of the 20s. I will always love this course, despite it's simplicity. -4 on the round (I think, can't be bothered to confirm with my phone sitting on my person).
- Home at 3:30. 
- Theodore to acting lessons for 4:00
- Pick up L and Aaron from daycare.
- Home again by 4:20
- Supper
- Evening work
- Evening outdoors in the dark (!) with the kids... a short trip to the shed for bike pads and a Kylo Ren helmet (L led this excursion). I putted a few times.
- Learned about and wrote notes about Strings in java... for hours. Need to play some serious catch-up with work during this 'break'.

The memory of that guy coming up to me in Walmart still rattles me throughout the day. Now, for example. 




2021-11-09 11:50 PM:  noted today during my rounds that dumbbell exercises have had a noticeable effect on my DG game. Keep with it. 


2021-11-12  2:16 PM:  I need to write more. Feelings require production. I feel so many things part way. 

They may not even be of myself—these feelings. Don't you understand that?! 

Feelings fully sussed out can be throw out, they can be acknowledged in the moment and forgotten about, or they can become polished, concise, self-constructed guides to living a considered life. 
Either way, they guide you—me. Feeling only part way gets you (me, again) nowhere. 

That's really how I feel. Nowhere. This is one feeling that I have polished with all the turds of partial feelings. 

Put yourself to producing. Produce with meaning and with feeling. Put yourself into your work, because you are the work. How little you care about that shows how little you care about yourself and everyone in your life. 




2021-11-12  2:42 PM:  watching Tootsie for the first time. 1982. A year before I existed.

The 'Jesus' when shutting the window on the nighttime serenade. Great delivery. Hilarious. 

Ugh. Typing on a phone is torture. 


2021-11-13 12:05 AM:  you're saying that, now and then, I'll be consoled. 

Portrait of a Lady on Fire


2021-11-13 12:09 AM:  this movie makes me want to interact more with the world. 


2021-11-13  1:18 AM:  attraction. passion. tears.


2021-11-13  1:36 AM:  Intense ending. 

A movie of looks. 

Oh, it's such a great movie. Committed to isolation. Ends with isolation of the most common kind—isolation among the crowds.


2021-11-13  9:59 PM:  The Matrix. Found a blu ray copy at _Downtown Entertainment_ today (I should write separately about that place).
