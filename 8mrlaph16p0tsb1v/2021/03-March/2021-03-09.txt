## iA Writer

Freedom in restriction. When using iW, I don’t worry about font choices or extensibility. I simply write.

The application is like a fine notebook. The interface is attractive, the small details are finely crafted. 

It makes me want to write. 

Why have I stopped using it? Well, the first time was because I no longer used a Mac. I first purchased iA Writer on the iPad Mini (1st gen). When I moved to a MacBook Air (2014 model), I dabbled in software that aimed to do more. 

I tested Scrivener on a novel writing month...

But moving to those pieces of software resulted in me doing less writing.
