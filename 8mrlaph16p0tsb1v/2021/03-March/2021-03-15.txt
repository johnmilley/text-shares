## Wonder without Stress

It is so lovely to learn difficult things. Teaching requires that you learn and re-learn difficult things and it is often not always a lovely experience, oftentimes, of late, it has been mind-numbing and all-consuming. I walk through days of my life consumed with skills not yet learned, theoretical knowledge not understood, and assessments unrealized. My reaction and oft-lack of preparedness for my job is destroying the wonder and joy that learning aught to bring.
