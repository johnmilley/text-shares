### Theodore's Party

Theodore's party was yesterday at Bartlett Point Park. I set up a disc golf course (3 holes) as the activity for the kids. The top players, Ross, Noah, and Theodore really took to it which was great to see. Lauren and Marianne had fun with it as well (Lauren was only +2, one more than the top 3, all at +1). Noah won the prize (a Deputy putter that Theodore picked out online) after parring hole 1, the first of the play-off.

Most of the parents kind of goofed off away from the party, which was fine. Riley's just took off, which I thought strange. Riley was a little whiny and wanted more of Theodore's attention than he was getting. Theodore played so well with Noah and Ross that it came off as kind of pathetic, which is a terrible thing to think about his best friend, but... well, it was.

Gifts: $25 Chapters card from Donna and Mohamed, $25 Nintendo eShop card from Riley, $25 from Ross, and the best ever Spiderman suit from us. Theodore put the suit on at the end of the party. Best $29 USD ever spent. He really does look like a Theodore-sized Spiderman. It's incredible.

I may have laid the Disc Golf on a little thick this year (mini discs in the loot bags, and a mini basket to be given on his birthday), but they had fun with it and took to it immediately. GOTTA GROW THE GAME!
