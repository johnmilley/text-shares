## Some thoughts

**Location: Woody Point**
**Time: August 26, Morning**

This has been a different month. I am finally trying to better myself. There have been many ups and downs because of that—sookiness and withdrawals with a stingy sprinkling of ... hope? Maybe.

ok... I am not writing quickly enough. Focusing too much on how the words read. This ain't for reading. It's a ritual bleeding, a cleansing of old shitty habits. This is a reckoning.

I no longer drink every night. That is not the person I am and it is not the person I ever wish to be again. I want to engage in my habits and hobbies fully and soberly. As the celebrity-worshiping self-help book I am reading states—I want to *thrive in sobriety*.

So here I am, on August 26th, at the *Rooms at Woody Point*, writing my heart out.

I have missed writing about some moments this month, so I am going to use this space here to do it.

### Catching Fish

How the fuck is this done? I remember the feeling as a kid of reeling in a fish. I have not been able to provide Theodore with that feeling yet and it is gutting me. This summer has been one of forgetting to buy the damn worms and instead relying on Walmart bait. This is a wrong that must be righted before the summer ends.

I am planning on taking Theodore and Marianne to St. John's before the summer ends (Rebecca will stay with L, put him in daycare and get some work done). I should bring the rods and have a go at the gullies in the Goulds. That would be a proper place to catch his first.

### Birthdays

The birthday season is finally over.

**Theodore:** Party at Bartlett Point Park. Disc golf, playground, pizza, Spiderman. Good times and a happy, social boy. Theodore has come so far in the last year in his social behavior. So proud of him. 

On his birthday, I assembled our new BBQ, we had a water balloon fight, and I grilled burgers. Riley joined us. Oh, I made the cupcakes... which were pretty great.

**Marianne:** I haven't written much about Marianne's birthday yet. Busy time with the parents in town.

Her party was at the Marble Inn Pool. Theodore had a game with the traveling team at 1:30. They practiced for 30 minutes and then started playing. Marianne's party started at 3:00. I ran up to him after he finished a long shift in net to yank him from the game. That was close to 3. The game ended up running until 3:20 or so. (He did a great job in net, making at least 3 big saves. The only goal he allowed was a beautiful 2-on-0 setup from second-cousin Maddie.

So, we got the party a little late, 3:10 or so. Not too bad.

I stayed and watched the kids swim for a little and then helped Dad and Mom set up the picnic tables and folding table for the party's second half. Greco party pizzas and a fantastic Rebecca cake. The strawberry icing was the big hit.

Lots of school friends at the party. We had never met them because of COVID. Taylor, McKenna (and her brother Calex), Cassie. Paige, Preston, Lauren, and Ross also joined. A good crew of kids. Marianne was beaming during her party.

### St. John's

The summer wouldn't feel right ending without a little more time in St. John's. Rebecca is going to stay home with Lawrence. He can go to daycare while she gets some time to get work done.

Right now the plan is to leave next Thursday (Sept 2) and drive back on Monday (Sept 6), giving us 3 full days in town. I want to take them to Bowring Park, Toys R Us, and downtown. I also want to play disc golf and see how low I can score now that I have been obsessing over the sport for the summer.

### The weeks ahead

Returning to work is always a shaky time for me. A huge part of the problem is that I think too much of my time off and I never end up achieving the things that I want to. I am starting to improve there, but there are always days and weeks of the summer where I feel like I should be getting more done. I never do and I tend to blame it on the family. However, it's entirely my fault. I don't put myself in the right mindset to get work done. The time is there, but really it is the will and drive that is missing. That's all on me.

Writing these words today, for instance. I have time to do this everyday, but I choose to do other things like mindlessly scroll through shit on my phone. I could have built a website or an application over the summer, but I chose not to. There is only so much time. If productivity was truly a goal of mine, then I would make time for it. But really, it is not. 

I work 9 months of the year. During those 9 months, I am plenty busy doing productive things (earning a salary, for example). I need to drop the hope of having professionally productive summers.

... ok I am rambling and not being super coherent (to myself).

COVID has given me (and most people) a tough year and a half. My desire to improve myself professionally has waned considerably. I have read very little in the past 2 years. I have created few things. I often feel like a failure, contrary to outside opinion and... well, fact. I have a good job and a wonderful family.

Teaching programming and web development makes me feel like I should be cranking out apps and products on my own time... to build my credibility (to myself? to others?). I get hung up on not having a desire to code big applications, when really my job does not require that. I put too much pressure on myself, obviously. I am trying to let that go, but it's difficult.
