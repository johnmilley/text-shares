# Goodbye, Elswick Road

I had the idea last night after smoking a sativa pre-rolled from Tweed (cannabis become legal while we lived in this home) that as I am moving about the house I should jot down whichever memories are sparked in the process. Here they are. Along with some moments of the moving day.

## Saturday, May 8: kids slept in HV, R and I packing the rest of our possessions.

**Some memories**

- While I will be mostly happy to leave our short-ceilinged basement behind, T and I had some moments down there in Summer 2020: building his workbench, teaching him how to saw a board, how to hammer a nail. Marianne and I spent some time down there early this winter, putting together a wooden mail-order toy kit — a candy dispenser.
- T lived here from 4—7, M from 2–5, L from 0–3, R from 34–37. Me, 35–38.
- The beginning of family meetings in 2020. We have been far from perfect in our consistency with a lot of things in this home, but a lot of good things started here and will continue throughout our lives.
- The poor view from my seat at the kitchen table. The view : the side of Dirk and Alli's house and their backyard. A small, but appreciated, chunk of the sky (today that sky is grey).
- A lot of crafting has been done on this kitchen table and a lot more will be done in our new home.
- Staring out the front window, past the overhang on the deck to try and get a view of the kids. If no sight, crack the window to get a sound.
- Playing 'mud' on the top landing. Pillows and blankets from beds formed the muddy ground.

**Notes of the day**

- Johnston came over and took apart the trundle bed that Theodore had been using for the past 2 years. It was on loan from them.
- Made a solo run to the house to pack the pantry and load up the van with the contents of the 'playroom' (Lego, toys, TV). Picked up Chicken Adobo from Wowo to eat in HV. Delicious.
- Dirk came in and dismantled the box spring that would not fit up the stairs. His first time in our house...which is shocking.

## Sunday, May 9: We all slept in HV.

**Some more memories**

- I'll miss our long paved driveway. Theodore and I played our last ball hockey there on Thursday. Theodore learned how to ride his bike there. Sunk his first basket. 
	- 12 Mac doesn't have a paved driveway. It's an upgrade that we are considering.


**Some more notes **

- Taking the Boston branded pencil sharpener from the basement as a memento.
- Waited until too late to order Chinese food. Mary Browns for Mother's day supper.
- Weather the past few days: rain, rain, rain. Miserable looking days.

## Monday, May 10: Moving day(s)

**any memories or is it just a mad rush?**
- mad rush
- memories are coming in at an equally rushed paced. a lot of wonderful moments in that house that was ours. 

**Notes**

- Movers: 4th feller popped by at 10:15 with a bag and again at 2:10 to help with the piano. $1500
- Rebecca finished cleaning the house 5 minutes before the new owner's were scheduled to do their final walk-through (1pm).
- I was with the truck most of the day. Elswick was loaded for Macpherson by 11. Unloading at Macpherson completed by 1:30. Back to Elswick for HV load that was placed outside on the deck by R and Johnston. HV unloaded by 2:20. Back to Macpherson and unloaded by 3.
- Great chats about the house with Doreen and Gord. Gord gave me the tour of his deck and explained his method for painting the house, the deck, and the stairs.
> - Cheque picked up from the lawyers by 4:30

# 05-20-2021: Limber edition

Joe DeFranco is going to save my life.

A number of years ago—let's say five years ago—I was in a usual rut of sorts. Eating poorly. Drinking too many beers. Etc. I restarted exercising in earnest at Forever Young Fitness in the Civic Center using my work discount—50% monthly membership.

During that months long weight training and cardio stint I was warming up with a program titled *Agile 8*. Widely touted on Reddit. It was a bit showy to be performing in the gym, but I had some good success with it. I have a deadlift video from back around that time. I was in pretty good shape. That may be the stint that ended with a poor deadlift and months of back pain.
