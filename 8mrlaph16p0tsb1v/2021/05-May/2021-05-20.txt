# 05-20-2021: Limber edition

Joe DeFranco is going to save my life.

A number of years ago—let's say five years ago—I was in a usual rut of sorts. Eating poorly. Drinking too many beers. Etc. I restarted exercising in earnest at Forever Young Fitness in the Civic Center using my work discount—50% monthly membership.

During that months long weight training and cardio stint I was warming up with a program titled *Agile 8*. Widely touted on Reddit. It was a bit showy to be performing in the gym, but I had some good success with it. I have a deadlift video from back around that time. I was in pretty good shape. That may be the stint that ended with a poor deadlift and months of back pain. 

Fast forward to now. It’s been a while since I lifted regularly. Pre-COVID at the very least. I miss it. Lately I have been experiencing a lot of lower back pain. That, coup led with my occasionally nagging knee injury from last summer, has put me in a bad spot. When searching for solutions yesterday, I stumbled upon an updated Agile 8—the Limber 11. 

Some of these exercises *hurt*. However, they feel necessary. Going to give it a go for a few weeks. I’m hopeful that it will start to address some long neglected muscular aches and immobility.
