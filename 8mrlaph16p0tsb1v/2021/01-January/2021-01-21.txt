# Jan-21-Stress

Feeling overworked. I am drained. Putting in a lot of effort to create new teaching materials. Not sure if the effort will be worth it. 

## Systems

My systems—and plans for system improvement—have collapsed. 

I am using iA Writer on my iPad to write this. I like this program, but it lacks automation. Perhaps that is a good thing. I have been getting to caught up in developing a system that I can use from anywhere and that can be centralized through automation—the last system used Drafts, org-mode, and Dropbox; the system before used Drafts and Dropbox.

The only good system is one that gets out of the way and lets me write. A fucking notebook is perfect. I can write anywhere on the page. I can bookmark it to pick up where I left off. I can pick it up and flip through it. Finding specific things is difficult, but how many times have I even wanted to do that? Once or twice for a recipe that I transcribed from a YouTube video.

A journal is an outlet. Words and images. Imprints. Markings put onto and into the page. Ink leaves my mark. What does this leave?

Rebecca is likely returning her new laptop. That means no old macbook for me. That's a bummer. Next year I'll correct the mistake.

Writing this entry was pleasant. iA Writer with focus mode. It gets out of the way. I like that.
