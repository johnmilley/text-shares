# Oh Sister - February 4, 2021

I finally responded to Jill. She emailed a wedding invite a few days after Christmas and I have avoided saying anything to her about it until today. This morning she messaged Rebecca to ask if we would be going.

No. We will not be going. 

Writing her back has filled me with tremendous stress. Stress that I unload on my family and on to myself. I am hoping that messaging her today is the end of it for a while, but I'm not really that foolish. 

Getting it out was cathartic, but it was also the start of a new truth, a turn in the relationship. I wrote her a letter while she was in prison. I expressed many of the same feelings today as I did then. Not much has changed in her behaviour. She continues to be a selfish manipulator. The way she treats our parents, mom especially, is painful to me. Mom and dad should be enjoying their post-dependent years. They have put in their time and still they get shit on daily by this brat of a woman. 

\*Sigh\*

Tomorrow begins the first break of the winter semester. No classes on Friday and Monday. A much needed break.

## Same shit, different day - Feb 5, 2021

Mom called Rebecca's phone this morning. I took the call as I was about ready to head out to daycare with L. Rebecca was on a walk.

Mom and dad got a response from the application they filed for custody. Jill is going all in with the lies. Says that other than the time she was incarcerated, she has been the primary decision maker and has always been so for schooling and medical appointments. 

I have been silently raging all day. It's 1:30pm now and I have wasted most of my 1 of 2 prep days. 

Date: February 6, 2021 at 00:00:00 AM NST



# Radiohead's True Love Waits and True Love

(--BEGIN-- 	)

Love of children, of wife, of friends, of family. 

I am thinking about the after-, momentary-, and under-affects of love. 

There is the grateful love of having loved when love was there. Love seized, etc.

There is the momentary love. Love in the moment and of the moment. It is realized and wonderful, but fleeting.

Then there is the affecting and aching of love owed and left unpaid. This love aches in the deepest parts of your soul. No wonder. You had love, space, and time and you blew it. Time wasted. Time disrespected. Time lost.

Missed opportunities with the kids hurts hardest these days. A bad day turns into a bad week, a bad month. It's never too late to be better, you tell yourself. You know—you ought to know—that it could be. It's a moment away. You must know that. You have to know that.

Just don't leave.
Don't leave.

You plead for them not to grow up faster than you can improve your love. My imperfect love.

# Feb-7-Manic_Python

My first semester as lead instructor in the Software Development program is going just about how I pictured it. High stress, little to no direction. Things are getting a little better? Tomorrow will be an important day for planning the remainder of this month in all my courses.

This is my first semester teaching a course in Python. I am learning a lot, but at the sake of many things: sanity, quality time with family, sleep. It has been rough going trying to balance it all, but I think I am entering a better phase now.

# Feb-9-Kids-Important-Things

Watching WandaVision Ep1. Old MacDonald Had a Farm E I E I O.

Lawrence loves to sing. Mary Had a Little Lamb is another favourite. He love music. He needs an instrument. I think I'll pick up a modern Kid Casio keyboard for his birthday.

He likes to play with the tone and tempo of the songs he knows. He seems to understand that they are 'made up things.' Knowing this is empowering him to make things up. Beautiful to see it unfold. 

Theodore traded Positivity Paws for 2 Tim Horton's Carey Price hockey cards. What an interesting and fun thing for his teacher (Chrissy Payne) to do. He was waiting to tell me when I got home from work.

He showed me the heavily creased cards. Nice cards, foiled, classic poses. He explained that they had been in his pocket. He treats Pokemon cards the same way. To him, cards are meant for the pocket. Their shape does suggest that. 

I took out one of my two surviving binders of hockey cards and let him browse them in the downstairs room. There was a Patrick Roy holographic sticker card that he particularly liked. I took it out of the plastic 3x3 sleeve for the first time in...  23 years? That's a guess, but it may be longer. It's a great hologram — it moves from silver to gold to full colour within millimetres of movement.

I picked up a couple of 60-card Pokemon starter decks on clearance at Walmart to play with him. 

Books: Myself, I am reading through *The Death of Ivan Illych* for the second time (it's been at least 15 years). T's book is Lucy and Andy Neanderthal Book 2 that he himself purchased on my Kindle (We had read the sample one night and the next day I receive an email informing me of my recent Kindle Book purchase... he acts dumb, but I know he knew... and it makes me proud). He is a beautiful kid. Filled with anxiety, as was I (and am I), but so so wonderful. Smart, funny. Interesting. 

Ok, yeah. Maybe these are the interesting things to write about. Maybe my own continuing seasonal shortcomings are boring. Boring to me at least. My problems blah blah. 

No. My kids. Their lives. Our lives with them. Those are the interesting things.

Modern Family.

Writing for posterity. Adding context to lives lived.

#Feb-13-On-a-Wire

Sunday, February 14, 2021 at 12:08:21 AM

I believe I have just given my best performance of Bird on the Wire by Leonard Cohen. I was the only one to hear it. I channelled him and felt him with me the whole way through.

I think back to that night at Holy Heart. He was still giving it his all in his 70s. Recapturing something thought lost, or something that he was losing. A beautiful, vulnerable man. Laid bare and applauded. We wanted him and he gave himself to us. Blissful evening.

Lovely times with the kids tonight playing Roblox (Adopt Me - a game where you choose to be a baby or a parent and you adopt pets that hatch from eggs. Uh huh). Marianne was first up. I had fun and followed her lead. Belly laughs and empowerment. Theodore could not help but creep down the stairs to look at us. 

When it was his turn he said, "I am over Fortnite, I want to play this." I had to muster up the same enthusiasm. It wasn't difficult. We had a great time. It was now Marianne's turn o wait on the stairs, unable to resist being a part of the fun.

Rebecca had a hard time with the unscheduled nature of it all. I get it. I don't blame her. 

But. The answer lay in what I did with them tonight. I felt equal to them and they responded to my efforts. 

Theodore came down to give me a hug goodnight after his bath bomb bath with his new Season 2 Beskar outfitted Mandalorian Lego figure.

... COVID gave me a good excuse to buy that for him this morning.

Picking up my office stuff — keyboard and webcam and books and notes — on Monday. Expected to resume classes as if nothing has happened. Uh huh. Going to have to give R a couple of hours out of each day. 

Tough time ahead, but we'll manage.
