# Feb-9-Kids-Important-Things

Watching WandaVision Ep1. Old MacDonald Had a Farm E I E I O.

Lawrence loves to sing. Mary Had a Little Lamb is another favourite. He love music. He needs an instrument. I think I'll pick up a modern Kid Casio keyboard for his birthday.

He likes to play with the tone and tempo of the songs he knows. He seems to understand that they are 'made up things.' Knowing this is empowering him to make things up. Beautiful to see it unfold. 

Theodore traded Positivity Paws for 2 Tim Horton's Carey Price hockey cards. What an interesting and fun thing for his teacher (Chrissy Payne) to do. He was waiting to tell me when I got home from work.

He showed me the heavily creased cards. Nice cards, foiled, classic poses. He explained that they had been in his pocket. He treats Pokemon cards the same way. To him, cards are meant for the pocket. Their shape does suggest that. 

I took out one of my two surviving binders of hockey cards and let him browse them in the downstairs room. There was a Patrick Roy holographic sticker card that he particularly liked. I took it out of the plastic 3x3 sleeve for the first time in...  23 years? That's a guess, but it may be longer. It's a great hologram — it moves from silver to gold to full colour within millimetres of movement.

I picked up a couple of 60-card Pokemon starter decks on clearance at Walmart to play with him. 

Books: Myself, I am reading through *The Death of Ivan Illych* for the second time (it's been at least 15 years). T's book is Lucy and Andy Neanderthal Book 2 that he himself purchased on my Kindle (We had read the sample one night and the next day I receive an email informing me of my recent Kindle Book purchase... he acts dumb, but I know he knew... and it makes me proud). He is a beautiful kid. Filled with anxiety, as was I (and am I), but so so wonderful. Smart, funny. Interesting. 

Ok, yeah. Maybe these are the interesting things to write about. Maybe my own continuing seasonal shortcomings are boring. Boring to me at least. My problems blah blah. 

No. My kids. Their lives. Our lives with them. Those are the interesting things.

Modern Family.

Writing for posterity. Adding context to lives lived.
