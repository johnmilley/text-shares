# Radiohead's True Love Waits and True Love

Love of children, of wife, of friends, of family. 

I am thinking about the after-, momentary-, and under-affects of love. 

There is the grateful love of having loved when love was there. Love seized, etc.

There is the momentary love. Love in the moment and of the moment. It is realized and wonderful, but fleeting.

Then there is the affecting and aching of love owed and left unpaid. This love aches in the deepest parts of your soul. No wonder. You had love, space, and time and you blew it. Time wasted. Time disrespected. Time lost.

Missed opportunities with the kids hurts hardest these days. A bad day turns into a bad week, a bad month. It's never too late to be better, you tell yourself. You know—you ought to know—that it could be. It's a moment away. You must know that. You have to know that.

Don't leave.

You plead for them not to grow up faster than you can improve your love. My imperfect love.
