# The Last of Us Part II

Man. What a few days. I blew through the last 15 or so hours of the game in the past 4 days or so.

It was an emotionally charged experience. I wish Ellie and Abby spoke to each other. That was too much to expect from Ellie. She is a lost soul. How could she not be?

At first, I was disappointed that I would be spending so much time with Abby, but she was such a great character. Her redemption arc was believable. 

I was on fire during the last Ellie sequence. Destroying all in my path. Headshot after headshot. Seeing Abby 2 months into captivity was heartbreaking.

... maybe a lighter game next.
