# 2020-11-13: why do anything

*November 13/14, 2020*

**My day-dream answer to a question about songwriting—why do it at all?**

I just write them and sing them and put them out there.  
I don't expect anything of them... not yet. Maybe someday I will.  
But right now, honestly, talking to you is the most I've thought about them.  
I sung them months ago.  
...  
I have no audience to keep me moving.  
There is no intimacy. 
I write and I forget, I write and I forget—but sometimes I hit on a note.
An abrupt and pleasant surge radiates. My heart is triumphant, for a moment.  
A sweet moment. 

> I don't know why I do this, but I do know why I keep going.
