# August

the horses are coming down violin road

listening to Tom Waits with a guitar in hand while I learn how to write in IA Writer for Windows.

It feels like the right purchase with all my new sold goods. It's good to get shit out. Drawing and writing do that. Demons, angels—it all comes to the surface. At least it comes to the finger tips. I reach for that backspace key too often. Backspace. My safe space. Coward. Yeah, that hurts but it's the truth.

gone to Indiana  
never coming home

## 08

All the remaining money from my decluttering now goes into the family pot of money. This needs to happen. I am not good with money. I am bad with money. I spend what I don't have. I spend what *we* don't have.

## 13

    08/13/20: 09:39 It's the morning after the party. Moving in slo mo. Heading out with L soon to the pirate ship park and to meet up with P, T, and M. 
	
I got the *Magic Keyboard* today 😄

Going to take time to get used to the size of the keyboard, but the keys are lovely to type on. The perfect click. Trackpad is small but nice to touch. Super responsive. Ok. Enough gushing. Time to get back to cleaning up my physical and digital life. 

Today I made the switch from Dropbox to iCloud because this program (iA Writer) works more seamlessly with iCloud. Will store photos + videos there in addition to Google. Price for 200GB with the two services = $8 a month. Fair price.

I am going to type so much garbage on this thing, but that means that I will be typing. That’s the goal. 

Focus mode highlights only the sentence that you are working on. Why is it bouncing around so much? Ok, that has stopped. Weird.

Remapped Caps Lock —> Esc key. This keyboard has none. It doesn’t have Function keys either. No big deal.

LOVE IT!!!!!!

**Sitting on the couch:** Doesn’t have the stability of a laptop. Works better on a table, but the typing experience is still pretty great. 

**Lying down:** not bad. I can type. I can see the screen. Again, not the ideal way to use it. Works better than the lap.

I’ll take this for a serious ride over the next few days when I attempt to make iA templates for work.

**Park + Lunch**

Lawrence had a great time at the ‘pirate ship park’ (as M calls it). There is a big ship and a train with 4 or 5 cars, all wooden, at the base of the adult climbing playground. Whatever that structure is in front of marble. L did about 10 loops of the wood structures. Walking up and down the ramps of the ship and through and over the train cars. 

Dad was having a Zoom meeting back at the room they had at the Marble Inn and Suites. We ate lunch there. Pizza for the kids. Chicken Pesto sandwich and Green Machine Smoothie (w/ seaweed!). It was yum. Much better than the poor experience we had at Best Coast Cafe yesterday with Theodore.

**Hamburgers**

Lori and the kids popped over this afternoon. Mom and dad and Preston are here. We all ate burgers and apple pie. 

**Thought** I get sad at times when I realize that it’s people at al levels.  Every decision is made by a person and that’s not a comforting thought. (That includes decisions made by our future robot overlords. Started by people...)

 **Drafts**
 
 I had hoped to be able to use drafts to insert quick notes throughout the day. Instead of doing that I could keep using it how I was and merge it into the monthly file at the end of each month. Write a script to do that. Maybe. Will think on it some more.
 
 It’s time to turn my attention to work and how I am going to use these new tools to do my job.
 
 Need to build templates that for the following:
 
 * Assignments (may not need one)
 * Class notes (can I make templates that are interactive like I created using VS Code? May be a overly complicated task. Will devote small amount of time to this next week.
 * Slides (?)

**New Tools**

Look at how much text I’ve written today. New tools!

This keyboard transforms the iPad completely. 

Ok. Enough journaling for one night...

## 14

Back on the trails. Took a break while family were here. Ran a wimpy pipeline-gorge-west valley. Planning to head out again tomorrow. Still not completely up to running the unofficial Steep 50.

Mulling over whether or not to apply for the permanent SD job. I’m not sure that I want it. I enjoy teaching the CSN students and really enjoy teaching Linux. I think I bring a different and welcomed perspective to that program and would prefer that the main software instructor was taught by someone who has more experience in industry. It may well be the wrong decision not to apply, but I am happy where a currently am. Live in the present, etc.

+++

**Evening**

A lazy evening. Spaghetti for supper. Leftover chocolate birthday cake for dessert. Montreal are winning 4-0 with 12:41 left in the 3rd. Time keeps ticking down.

Starting to feel those back-to-work blues creep in. Going to try and prevent a full blown bout of nerves this time around. Online classes may help with that. So might this snazzy sounding and feeling keyboard of mine. Some snazzy.

**Minecraft Dungeons**

We failed and we succeeded in Minecraft Dungeons this evening. Game over on “The Big Red Guy”, victory in discovering the hidden temple. NOT SO HIDDEN NOW ARE YOU TEMPLE.

It’s a lot of fun. Theodore is learning about loot.

**Paper Journals**

What will I use my paper journals for? I like the type of record that they become. More so than this text. This feels like a brain dump. Physical notebooks have something special that digital just doesn’t have.
