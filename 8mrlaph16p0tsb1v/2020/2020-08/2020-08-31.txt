**Paper Journals**

What will I use my paper journals for? I like the type of record that they become. More so than this text. This feels like a brain dump. Physical notebooks have something special that digital just doesn’t have.

This is a productivity device. It’s a tool. It can contain parts of who I am, but not in the way that a notebook does. The notebook itself is a big part of that. It’s the paper I chose to carry around and to fill. It can be seen wherever it is. It needs no operating system. It contains all it needs to be seen within itself. It is physical.

These are words have the idea of physicality. The characters exist in long strings of 1s and 0s. They are numbers. Each keystroke is converted to a number; that number is converted into a string of binary; 

Here I can write and write. With abandon. It takes up bytes.
