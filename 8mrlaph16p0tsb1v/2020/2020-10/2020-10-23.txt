### Jesus Christ. Life.

Reading this evening about the short life of Noah Myles. Family is first. It always has to be. Pam, Noah's mother, has the strength of the strongest humans. God. A word for the everything. Jesus. The embodiment of God.  Three letters. Five. Three letters as an answer to the biggest of questions. It has attraction.

![Oct 24, 2020 at 1:50 AM—Noah Myles](https://www.dropbox.com/s/t6h70zdfv8klc0b/IMG_0197-2.jpeg?dl=1)

Answering the big whats and whys frees up time for the every day duties of life. I can imagine a local family that lives these ideas and succeeds in ways because of it. It can be a winning strategy. History backs that up. Is it still a vocation that this generation can pick up? I see glimpses of pure goodness in my students, but most are in that large middle, where I place myself—that big large cohort of arseholes who life selfishly and carelessly, however kindly and good-naturedly.

Sin is in the life that I am living. Again, a word. Three letters to convey all of the evils that suck hours from my days and quality from my life, and the lives of others.
