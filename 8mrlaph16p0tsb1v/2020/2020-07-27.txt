Men often look for a saviour in a partner. Such expectations can and never should be met. A good relationship often boils down to a agreeable division of labour. Agreeableness in this case typically involves sacrifice, more so from the position of the one who takes on the greatest portion of emotional baggage that relationships require.

In my own experience, that falls to the woman. I take the form of an immovable object from time to time. With persistence (nevertheless), movement becomes possible. Growth of the person, and subsequently, the relationship, becomes possible. 

Both actions—that of moving and of being moved—take great feats of strength. Both come with their resentments, however deserved or not. The flower is admired and photographed in full bloom. The struggle to beauty is there though overlooked. The flower remembers. 

The day to day, each trip through the great machinery, is made easier with a plan of attack.
