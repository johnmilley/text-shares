[Pomodoro Technique - Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pomodoro_Technique)

> There are six steps in the original technique:
> 1. Decide on the task to be done.
> 2. Set the pomodoro timer (traditionally to 25 minutes).[1]
> 3. Work on the task.
> 4. End work when the timer rings and put a checkmark on a piece of paper.[5]
> 5. If you have fewer than four checkmarks, take a short break (3–5 minutes) and then return to step 2; otherwise continue to step 6.
> 6. After four pomodoros, take a longer break (15–30 minutes), reset your checkmark count to zero, then go to step 1.

I could use this for my evenings to some extent. 1 exercise pomodoro, 1 reading (i.e. 25 minutes devoted to each). That's an hour of an already short 1.5–2 hour evening. Oi.

Anyways. I am happy that I yoga'd. I **will** practice more regularly. The and aches and pains of my aging bones demand it.
