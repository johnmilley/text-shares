# September 23, 2020

**Sleep:** Up at 6:30. Tired tired tired.

**Food:** Leftover roast chicken. Made a couple of skillet pizzas with leftover sourdough. I think they turned out well. 500 degrees, about 20 minutes.

## Entries

**Moving House**

Is moving this year the right decision? We have been debating moving to 43 Sunset Boulevard  for a number of weeks now. It's draining. None of the houses on the market tick all of the boxes, but 43 Sunset comes close.

One thing that excites me about Massey Drive is the view of the stars. I remember spending time on the deck at Bellwood just looking at the stars. In the Fall months we had a great view of the Pleiades. 

![Sep 23, 2020 at 11:48 AM—definition of pleiades.](https://www.dropbox.com/s/f7zek3z3kuu1sbu/IMG_0182-2.jpeg?dl=1)

When we moved to Humber Village, I thought that we would have great views of the stars, but the surrounding hills and the tall trees on the property really limited the view. I can see about 5% of the sky on Elswick. I rarely see the stars these days. We don't even get much sun down here.

A more pressing need is space. Theodore is back to sharing a room with Lawrence, the basement is dark, cramped, and wet. We have 1 full-bath.

 2:43 PM: Our current plan this evening is to clean up the workshop area of the basement and to put in an offer of 400k (what I think the house is really worth). Our limit is 420k.


#### Exercise and Tomatoes

 8:47 PM: I was going to skip Yoga and go right to beer. But I didn't I made myself get my mat and did 15 minutes. I'm better for it. Needs to become a regular practice. 15 minutes is the minimum amount of time to get to any activity. I could do the whole pomodoro thing... without the tomatoes.

[Pomodoro Technique - Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pomodoro_Technique)

> There are six steps in the original technique:
> 1. Decide on the task to be done.
> 2. Set the pomodoro timer (traditionally to 25 minutes).[1]
> 3. Work on the task.
> 4. End work when the timer rings and put a checkmark on a piece of paper.[5]
> 5. If you have fewer than four checkmarks, take a short break (3–5 minutes) and then return to step 2; otherwise continue to step 6.
> 6. After four pomodoros, take a longer break (15–30 minutes), reset your checkmark count to zero, then go to step 1.

I could use this for my evenings to some extent. 1 exercise pomodoro, 1 reading (i.e. 25 minutes devoted to each). That's an hour of an already short 1.5–2 hour evening. Oi.

Anyways. I am happy that I yoga'd. I **will** practice more regularly. The and aches and pains of my aging bones demand it.

#### Journaling

Right now, I am in the early stage of developing and writing the journal. I am spending a lot of my time thinking about the structure and automation I can add to the journaling system. That needs to stop some time soon. I need to follow a typical software development approach to completing a project on time. Treat the journal as a project—an endeavour that has a start and end date. Normal operations—using the journal—must then take effect. Versioning can happen only after your product has been tested and used in normal operation.
