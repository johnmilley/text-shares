## Entries

**The Playstation 5**

Aidan sent a text last night to ask if I could reserve a Playstation 5 for him today. All I had to go on for a timeframe was the official release of "_sometime on the morning of September 25_." 

I remembered this morning a minute or two before my 9am alarm—reminding me of very this task—went off. Refreshing the relevant pages of redflagdeals, walmart.ca, and ebgames.ca provided no results, so I decided to drive to the Murphy Square EBGames for their 11am opening. 

I got there and was surprised that the line was so small—10–12 people social distanced, with the aura of camping out for something nerdy. To me—distant, dear, kin.

... ok i am high and i need to finish this up quickly to move on with my evening ... entering not-well-thought-out mode ...

I stood in line. Was cold. Ebgames owner/operator told us the pre-order would not be going live until 1:30 that afternoon. Armed with this new information, she also recommended that I go home and get a jacket. 

My destination was back to work. Back to work to get my sweatshirt, my iPad, my Project Management text book. Then to the house to get my summer folding chair—Behemoth of lawn chairs. I was doing this selflessly for my nephew. Filled with good reason.

Anyhow, I went back to EBGames and saw that the lineup had disappeared. I walked into the store and was added to a list version of the line that once existed. I assume, that due to that ol' pandemic, that the middle-aged woman in charge (again, I'm not sure if she would be the owner, the operator (both?). I just know that she's often _in charge_) wanted to not be responsible for any publicly visible gatherings. I like her.

She put me on the list, 15th or so. I went back to work to await my call. She did not know how many units they were authorized to sell, so the call could have gone either way. I went back to my office.

While there, I refreshed the previously mentioned web pages. A poster on RedFladDeals alerted the world (and me) that Walmart had the 2-controller bundle version of the disc PS5 up for pre-order. I jumped on it. $836 taxes in. 

I received an affirmative call from the EBGames woman-in-charge. I passed on my spot in the line.

Happy that Aidan will get to experience the feeling of getting a console at launch. That seems like a good gamer feeling to have in the ol' history bank of feelings. I do not think that I have ever had the pleasure! Virtual Boy, perhaps. Little pleasure there.


**Astral Chain**

I had the desire to play a video game for 2+ hours tonight. I choose Astral Chain and I was happy with that decision. Fun game. Outlandish in a similar way as Bayonetta. The aesthetics are stunning. The legatus mechanic is challenging at first, but it clicks with a satisfying sense of accomplishment.
