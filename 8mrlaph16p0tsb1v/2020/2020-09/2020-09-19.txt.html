# September 19, 2020

**Sleep:**    Poor. Not enough. 5 hours, maybe.   
**Exercise:**    Disc golf  
**Reading:**    Drafts scripting docs, Automators Forum (get back to Ulysses tomorrow!)

---

## Entries

 3:32 PM: 

(As I wait on the curb at the Stephenville pool)

Thinking a lot and tinkering with automation. I would really like to start incorporating it into my work for building and grading assignments. 

Templating is an easy first step. Creating stylized notes, assignments, and other documents is not a difficult task. HTML may be the better option? Not something that I can focus too much on now while in the thick of it. Something to start seriously tinkering with next Spring during Intercession.

I need a system in place for Fall 2021. At that time I will likely be running the show. Need a system that covers all aspects — teaching, grading, admin tasks (identify them), co-op placements (though I'm hopeful that work will fall mostly to the co-op staff member). 

It's a big task, but a worthwhile pursuit. _We are only as good as our systems_. I'm seeing that this month with our new budget setup. 

A good system requires interaction, so ease of interaction is a key to creating a good system. The best system leverages the tools that I already have. I'm hopeful that the iOS environment will continue to be a good option.

**Idea**: slides compiled from notes (write both in the same document , but do so in a way that the slides can be pulled out and compiled using a script)

## Brief Notes
 9:04 AM:  Plan for the couple of hours I have free this morning: change outlet in kitchen, grocery shop, read a few pages of Ulysses, yoga/run. 


12:31 PM:  Only time for lunch after grocery shopping. Well. Lunch and sitting down. Watching the beginning of Intersteller. 


 3:29 PM:  Just finished a round of disc golf with Johnston at the new Stephenville course, Blanche Brook. +8. Fun times. Waiting outside the pool for my family. 


 6:42 PM:  Home from our annual Stephenville swimming trip. Felt lean and in the best swimming shape of my life in the pool. A surprisingly noticeable change from the last time I was in a pool — probably 8-10 months ago. **Supper**: Mary Browns rots the guts yet we keep going back to it. 


 7:49 PM:  Got my first emerald! And then minutes later got another. Minecraft daddy time with T.


11:03 PM: Erin and Johnston invited us to a virtual escape room with Erins parents and brother. We completed it in 52 minutes, 10 better than average. Nice production value. A strange but good way to spend an evening.
