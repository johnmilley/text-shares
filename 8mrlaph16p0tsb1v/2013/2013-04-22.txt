April 22

It's Monday, my 3rd day in Bordeaux, France.  I went for a run in the park this morning and in the afternoon I went to the Casino supermarche.  They have a self-checkout... beautiful.  Understanding and responding in French is proving a lot more difficult than my time in Russia with Russian.  Maybe doing a degree in the language alongside that visit helped... 

--------
April 24

Yesterday Rebecca and I explored downtown Bordeaux for the last time.  We bought 2 expensive stuffed cats and then went indoors at a cafe to escape the sun (we being the only ones thinking the sun needed escaping from).  From there we got ice cream from a colourful Italian place.  We sat in the nearby green space and made quick work of the desserts.  Upon returning to home base (Centre Prevost), I checked my term grades.  I'm now a Bachelor of Science!  Happy day.  Tomorrow we head west to see some sand.

--------
April 25th

Today we went to see the Dune of Pylla (?) and the city of Arcachon. Paul, a supervisor of Gabriel's, drove us in his tiny Twingo.  Sandy, a visiting professor from Slovenia, also joined us.  Massive pile of sand, hot beach, sunburned wife.  Thus was the day.  

---------
April 27

We arrived in Espere yesterday evening.  The estate (Domain de Labarthe) is as I pictured it (credit to google street view).  Our hosts recommended a town for us to visit; Rocamadour.  We took the hour journey this morning.  Only fitting that the journey to a holy city built into a rock face would be terrifying.  My hands were sweaty as I let go the wheel at the first restaurant we saw (L'Esplanade).  I had duck breast on top of a buckwheat crepe filled with stinky goat cheese.  It was tolerable but I think my tolerance is at an end for this trip.

Loves roundabouts.  Country driving in France is a real treat and has been one of the highlights of this trip.
